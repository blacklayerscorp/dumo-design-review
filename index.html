Commenting works like Figma

How to leave feedback

  1. Press C or tap “Comment”The button is at the bottom left of every page. On a phone it sits on the left edge.
  2. Click the exact spotType what should change. A numbered pin stays on that spot and that screen.
  3. Reply, resolve, reviewOpen “Feedback” to see every comment across all pages and jump straight to each one.
  4. Press “Send feedback”Send it by email or GitHub, or download a file for WhatsApp. We import it and see every pin where you left it.

Comments are saved in your browser until you send them. If you switch phone or browser, send what you have first.

Decisions
