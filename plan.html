Summary

A TikTok-style vertical video app for South African creators: post short videos, grow a following, and watch a test-money wallet fill up from views and gifts. Real payouts, iOS, LIVE and the Shop come in later phases.

Architecture

The app never talks to the database directly. Every request goes through the API, which checks auth and roles. Video goes straight from the phone to the video service and plays back from a CDN.

Solid lines are API calls. Dashed lines are video traffic. On a phone, scroll the diagram sideways.

Data model sketch

Postgres with foreign keys and soft-delete flags. Deleting an account removes the user's videos from Mux and storage through a background job (POPIA right to erasure).

Business rules

These rules drive both the wallet and the spreadsheet check in Milestone 4. Values marked "assumption" need the client's confirmation (see Questions).

Qualified view

Watched for at least 3 s, by someone who is not the creator, and not the same viewer on the same video within 30 minutes.

Assumption · confirm
View earnings

(qualified views ÷ 1,000) × the admin's rand rate, rounded to the cent and credited to the ledger daily.

Rate set in admin
Gifts

Viewers send gifts with free test coins. The creator's wallet shows 70% of the gift's rand value, and 30% goes to the platform ledger.

From brief
Age

Date of birth at sign-up. Under 13 cannot join. Ages 13–17 can watch and post, but their wallet is locked with an "Earn from 18" message.

From brief
No real money

No payment SDK in the build. Cash out shows "Coming soon", and every rand amount is labelled test money.

From brief
For You ranking

score = likes × 2 + comments × 3 + qualified views ÷ 10, decayed by age (half-life 24 h). Videos from blocked users are never shown.

Assumption · confirm

Risks & mitigations

RiskLevelMitigation
Video streaming costs grow faster than expectedHighCap at 720p, use a low-bitrate default on cellular, preload only 1–2 videos, set budget alerts at 50/80/100%, review the cost dashboard weekly.
OTP SMS delivery or cost problems in SAMediumTest on Vodacom, MTN, Cell C and Telkom in M1. Rate-limit OTP requests. Keep Clickatell as a fallback. Allow a "resend code" after 30 s.
POPIA compliance and data leaving SAMediumData and compute in Johannesburg. Collect only what we need. Sign a data processing agreement with the video provider (s72). Account deletion really deletes. Privacy policy agreed with the client.
Minors' safety (13–17 year olds)HighAge gate at sign-up, earning locked under 18, reporting and blocking everywhere, admin removal in under a minute, community guidelines accepted at sign-up.
Slow performance on low-end AndroidMediumTest on budget devices weekly, use HLS with a small first segment, cache thumbnails, profile in release mode, set a 2 s start budget in QA.
Scope creep from features the prototype shows (PayShap, Subscribe, LIVE, Go Global, cross-posting)HighThese are marked "Coming soon" or hidden in the pilot design and listed as decisions in Questions. Any change needs a written change request with price and time.
Google Play policy for user-generated content appsMediumPolicy requires in-app reporting, blocking, moderation and a published content policy, all built in M5. We complete the data safety form and content rating questionnaire early.
Slow client acceptance holds up payments and the scheduleLowTest scripts sent with every build, 15-min demo calls, a 5-working-day window, and acceptance overlapping the next milestone.
Company accounts not ready (Play, cloud billing, D-U-N-S)MediumAccount checklist in week 1. The client owns every account, and we are invited with access only.

Cadence, quality and Definition of Done

Communication

  • Written update every Monday and Thursday: done, next, blockers
  • 15-minute demo call at the end of each milestone
  • Feature questions go to BP Mhlanga before we guess
  • Code pushed to the company's GitHub at least twice a week

Acceptance

  • The client has 5 working days to test each milestone
  • Bugs found are fixed before payment
  • Payment is released through Fiverr milestones once every "done when" test passes
  • Scope changes are agreed in writing, with price and time, before work starts

Definition of Done

  • Matches the signed-off design
  • Strings are in the ARB file, with no hard-coded text
  • No secrets in code, and CI is green
  • Tested on the device matrix and on throttled 4G
  • README updated, libraries listed with their licences

Test devices

  • Samsung Galaxy A05 (entry level, Android 13/14)
  • Samsung Galaxy A15 (mid-range, our 2 s benchmark)
  • Xiaomi Redmi 12
  • Hisense or Infinix (low-end)
  • One Android 9 device (minimum version)

QA checks per milestone

  • Throttled 4G profile: video start ≤ 2 s on the A15
  • 15-minute swipe, like and comment session with no crashes (Crashlytics)
  • Wallet results match the client's spreadsheet to the cent (M4)
  • Report, then remove in under 60 s, timed (M5)
  • Data use measured with Data Saver on and off (M3)

Final handover checklist

  • All code in the company GitHub, latest version pushed
  • README covers setup, running locally, releasing and costs
  • Every account and service listed, owned by the company, your access removed
  • Recorded code walkthrough delivered
  • Signed confirmation that all work belongs to Dumo The Fame (Pty) Ltd

Security & ownership

Dumo The Fame (Pty) Ltd owns everything: code, designs, databases and accounts. Google Play, Google Cloud, Mux, the SMS provider and GitHub are opened in the company's name and paid by the company, and the team only gets access. No passwords, API keys or secrets in the repository: they live in Secret Manager and GitHub Actions secrets. Admin actions are written to an audit log. Only open-source libraries with commercial-friendly licences (MIT, Apache 2.0, BSD) are used.
